03

My biggest early weakness is now a strength

The clearest proof of growth isn't that I stopped making mistakes — it's that the category of mistake changed. Here's the single clearest before → after.

2023 · the weakness

Unsynchronized data races

My token manager was a mutable static var shared singleton whose state was touched across threads with no lock — and some completion paths fired neither success nor failure, so the app could silently hang. The ambition was right; the safety wasn't.

now · the strength

A single-writer lock contract

Today, documented lock-based concurrency is one of my strongest areas: Mutex-guarded state with a single-writer contract, explicit success/failure on every path. Same instinct — done right.

The singleton was never the real mistake — a token manager should have one source of truth. What I learned was to make shared state safe. Understanding why it was wrong mattered far more than simply never doing it again.
